## 5. MACHINE MANAGEMENT

### ▶ MANAGEMENT

![](4c2b7514cb446ac7896a9977fd780c2ea323530f17a431b58de6f2a0fdadaef0.jpg)

In the management menu, you can see emergency notifications, check error-handling situations and manage consumables.

### ▶ Emergency notification

- Rollover

When the machine rolls over, a notification will be delivered.

- Geofence

When the machine leaves geofence,
A notification will be delivered.

### ▶ Error

- Error
When error occurs, a notification will be delivered.

### ▶ Maintenance

- Consumable lifespans are sorted into 3 levels: Change / Check / Good.

### ▶ EMERGENCY NOTIFICATION

![](c0192d11043961c9124cba767a24ecf4ec576a36b8e0eba72d862824190e852a.jpg)

You can see a history of emergency events such as rollover and geofence.

⚠ When emergency events occurs, push message will be delivered.
#### ERROR

![](8d782d101e1d2d1c37fb855197613878b1e39d0d9775fd283a3b297d8f5f29ad.jpg)

You can see a list of occurred errors and the progress of troubleshooting in Error details.

### ▶ Error-handling procedure

① Occurred

② Accepted

③ Repairing

④ Resolved

### ▶ CONSUMABLES

![](ae1b5035df67f25daddeeb99439f4874258bbb814aecc4f7cf48766078fbfb90.jpg)

Lifespans of consumables are indicated in 3 levels: Change, Check and Good.

⚠ When consumables need replacement, push message will be delivered.
### ▶ REPLACEMENT HISTORY

Tap ‘History’

^{①}

icon on the maintenance menu to show the replacement history of consumables.
