## 4. STATUS OF MACHINE

### ▶ MACHINE STATUS

![](4c5d532ab47e0349de74889a379dc9f655392c3777e9566a579cdff60a884754.jpg)

⚠️ Displayed machine status items may vary depending on machine model.

Following machine status items are displayed.

① Battery voltage

② Fuel level

③ Coolant temperature

④ Parking brake

⑤ Shuttle direction

⑥ PTO

⑦ Air cleaner

⑧ Transmission oil temperature

⑨ Urea level

⑩ Engine hours

⚠ Items updated real-time:

Parking brake, shuttle direction, PTO, air cleaner

⚠ Items updated every 1 minute:

Battery, fuel level, coolant temperature, transmission oil temperature, urea level

⚠ Items updated every 1 hour:

Engine hours

#### BATTERY

![](b8138ff11e1e69714e344d767cc87ee25fa1c6ec9de9e9400136519605926d2c.jpg)

Battery icon shows the current voltage of the battery

- Red: 11.5V or less

When battery icon becomes red, charge the battery.
### ▶ FUEL LEVEL

![](062f5236313cbfe6a7bd096c421b7c51616b2d91a4ddf9e5bd0a6f1e16ff3f45.jpg)

Fuel level icon shows the current fuel level.

• Red icon: 15% or less

When fuel level icon becomes red, fill the fuel tank.

### ▶ COOLANT TEMPERATURE

![](fb382da5ca981f7a85ba19da328fc016537b03175ecbaa0ab14f587be7c1cc6d.jpg)

Coolant temperature icon shows the current temperature of coolant. When the engine is off, OFF is shown instead of numbers.

• Red icon: Coolant is overheated.

When coolant temperature icon becomes red, It means the coolant is overheated. Check followings.

① Coolant level insufficient

② Radiator fin clogged

③ Fan belt tension

### ▶ TRANSMISSION OIL TEMP

![](d16872ea58ef7529a5435fa68c81a6949ebdfd7c937b418690c56a05a1defd7c.jpg)

Transmission oil temperature icon shows the current temperature of the transmission oil. When the engine is off, OFF is shown instead of numbers.

- Red icon:

32^{\circ}\mathrm{F}

or less

⚠️ In cold weather, engine pre-heating (idling) is required.

### ▶ UREA LEVEL

![](c7b2c43c01a7a45d8679b141a93f0e7c6c28ba7da24a171a0c4656d73e89154c.jpg)

Urea level icon shows the current urea level.

• Red icon: 25% or less

When urea level icon becomes red, fill the urea quickly.

Engine power will be reduced when urea level is under 10%.

### ▶ ENGINE HOURS

![](1d4b6411876fdb0c64ca9ec5f2c76762afef83b37b08e7e0beb3b91c09f7ab35.jpg)

Engine hours icon shows the total time of usage.
