![](924a8496e7e9ff0fc0c1fb454efbdcfbc443dfda41d77013bb732a23d6599cec.jpg)

Do not lift anything only with one side of the tractor. If so, the tractor may fall on its side.

Make sure to distribute the load evenly.

![](4df46562543c9b8d4e3744b8f61597e9d0bd5033c75fadcfe05b6751dcd9f232.jpg)

Keep the clearance between the rear wheels as large as possible for safety of the tractor.

![](e4f1e03f7841b59c83a356980a4ef2bfe5e8b270b2c79d49d80d14ed843b639e.jpg)

#### WARNING

- Do not let anyone ride a loader for work, such as spreading fertilizer.

He/she may fall off the loader, leading to an injury or even death.

- Always lower the loader to the ground before leaving the tractor.
