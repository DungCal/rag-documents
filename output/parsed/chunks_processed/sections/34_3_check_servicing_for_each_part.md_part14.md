## 3. CHECK & SERVICING FOR EACH PART



### ▶ VENT THE FUEL SYSTEM

The fuel system is vented via the electric fuel supply pump.

In order to ensure that no error messages are generated, no attempt should be made to start the system up whilst venting.

This process is carried out as follows:

- Ignition "ON"
The electronic fuel supply pump switches on for 20 seconds in order to vent the fuel system and build up the required fuel pressure.
Wait until the electric fuel supply pump is disconnected from the control unit.

- Ignition "OFF"
Repeat the process at least 2 times until the fuel system is vented.
