## 3. CONTROL INSTRUMENTS



### ▶ DRIVE MODE SET DIAL

![](63b9a6752015aff655e9ed6bc68c8612b3d518a37681a3d0266184842f0072b8.jpg)

Drive mode can be selected with this dial.

- Turn clockwise: Power drive
Main shift gear shifts at high engine RPM while APS is activated.

- Turn counter-clockwise: Eco drive
Main shift gear shifts at low engine RPM while APS is activated.
