This switch is to operate the head lamps, horn and turn signal lamps.

- Position lamp –
Turn the dial ⓐ to the position ①.

- Horn –
Press the tip of the switch in the arrow direction.

![](6391bdc0ff2cccdb8cf6710602545323b63a6e9219f3fc3ce6a134747ba85ede.jpg)

Follow the below to operate head lamp.

- High beam –
Turn the dial ① to the position ②.

- Low beam – Pull the lever up with the high beam activated.

![](889251e39a471618091005e9aaa98d5ee44aab67ece077faad4305c14a609e0a.jpg)

#### WARNING

- The high beam can obstruct the view of other drivers coming in the opposite direction on a road, leading to an unexpected accident.

![](7a9c34292a95f9b7bc3a0d6db48510996ea5322b3727e697560302ac728e78c9.jpg)

Follow the below to operate turn signal lamp while the electric circuit is on.

- Right turn signal – Push the lever ① to the direction ⓐ.

- Left turn signal – Pull the lever ① in the direction ⑥.

![](c578245a698cc4901912e312c1c2f21fb04262ca91be472fe2a9168c361abe16.jpg)

#### CAUTION

- This lever is not automatically returned to the neutral position. Therefore set it back to the neutral position after a turn.
#### DPF REGENERATION PROCESS AND LAMPS

This lamp is on during DPF regeneration process.

![](7eeeecd4d04547485d315ec4839dc6b3c0cde6bd7b4d1d8d81b2165d5fc42f7b.jpg)

#### WARNING

- Be careful with muffler and exhaust gas during DPF regeneration process since they are extremely hot.

![](92bffa2ebfc9652a32b2f8926e401ff30e3796c15fc60fa7331c7ecc4d8d87b4.jpg)

<1

^{st}

stage DPF regeneration warning>

If particle material is accumulated on diesel particulate filter(DPF) at certain level, DPF regeneration warning lamp will be on.

DPF regeneration warning lamp will be off as soon as DPF regeneration process is done.

![](a6654b0777eddd460efdf38b7c7f25c5f48adff078948ad60c40baa04bf9fee7.jpg)

<2

^{nd}

stage DPF regeneration warning>

Operation of the tractor without DPF regeneration after the DPF regeneration lamp is on for a while, the engine torque will be reduced by 25%.

![](2d85c0e2f3acecc30d05edf73919d1a7d6fbc9ef658fa8dfedb40d50915fb85c.jpg)

#### WARNING

- Perform DPF regeneration process when DPF warning lamp is on.
The engine RPM may go down until DPF regeneration process is done.
