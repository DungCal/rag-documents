| ENGINE GROSS HORSE POWER | IMPLEMENTATION DATE |
| --- | --- |
| >750HP | JAN 1, 2000 |

| 100HP > ENGINE POWER ≥ 50HP | JAN 1, 1998 |

| 25HP > ENGINE POWER > 0HP | JAN 1, 2000 |
